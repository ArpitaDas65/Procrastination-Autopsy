
# 🧠 Procrastination Autopsy

> **You're not lazy. You have a pattern.**

**Procrastination Autopsy** is a behavioral self-reflection web app that helps users understand **why they procrastinate**, rather than simply tracking how much time they waste.

Instead of saying:

> *“You procrastinated for 3 hours.”*

the app asks:

**“Why didn't you start?”**

It analyzes recurring blockers such as **fear of failure, unclear next steps, perfectionism, phone distraction, low energy, overwhelm, boredom, and unclear goals** — then turns those reflections into a personalized **Procrastination DNA**.

---

## ✨ Why This Project Is Different

Most productivity apps focus on:

* ⏱️ Time tracking
* ✅ To-do lists
* 📊 Productivity scores
* 🔥 Streaks

**Procrastination Autopsy focuses on the psychology behind the behavior.**

The goal isn't to make users feel guilty.

It's to help them discover:

> **“What keeps happening right before I procrastinate?”**

After multiple reflections, the app can reveal patterns like:

```text
YOUR PROCRASTINATION DNA

Fear of failure        31%
Unclear next step      26%
Phone distraction      19%
Task too difficult     14%
Low energy             10%
```

And turn that data into a meaningful insight:

> **Your biggest blocker isn't laziness. It's task ambiguity.**

## 🚀 Features

### 🔬 Procrastination Autopsy

Perform a quick behavioral investigation whenever you avoid starting a task.

The app asks:

* What were you trying to start?
* How long did you avoid it?
* What did you do instead?
* Why didn't you start?

---

### 🧩 Behavioral Blockers

Choose one or multiple reasons:

* 😵 Too difficult
* 😐 Boring
* 😨 Fear of failure
* 🧩 Don't know where to start
* 📱 Phone distraction
* 💤 Low energy
* 🎯 Goal unclear
* ✨ Perfectionism
* 😬 Overwhelmed

---

### 🧬 Procrastination DNA

Your reflections are aggregated into a behavioral profile.

The dashboard identifies your most common procrastination patterns and displays them visually through:

* Percentage breakdowns
* Animated progress bars
* Radial visualization
* Primary blocker
* Secondary blocker

---

### 📈 30-Day Behavioral Analysis

Track your recent procrastination behavior through:

* Autopsy count
* Number of patterns identified
* Starting speed
* Avoidance trends
* Activity heatmap

---

### 🧠 Trigger Map

Visualize the cycle behind procrastination:

```text
IMPORTANT TASK
      ↓
UNCERTAINTY
      ↓
OVERTHINKING
      ↓
AVOIDANCE
      ↓
DISTRACTION
      ↓
GUILT
      ↓
MORE AVOIDANCE
```

The goal is to identify where the loop begins.

---

### 💡 Personalized Next Move

The app doesn't stop at analysis.

It suggests a tiny, practical action based on your dominant blocker.

For example:

**Blocker:** Fear of failure

> Don't aim to do it perfectly.
> Write the worst possible first draft, on purpose.

**Blocker:** Task ambiguity

> Don't try to finish the project.
> Define the first 5-minute action.

---

### 📚 Autopsy History

Every investigation is saved locally and can be:

* 🔎 Searched
* 🏷️ Filtered by blocker
* ↕️ Sorted by date
* 🗑️ Deleted individually

---

### 🏆 Milestones

The app recognizes progress with milestones such as:

* First Autopsy
* Pattern Spotted
* 7 Days
* Self-Aware

---

### 🔐 Privacy First

No account is required.

Your reflections are stored locally in your browser using:

```text
localStorage
```

No backend.
No database.
No external API.

Your personal reflections stay on your device.

## 🧪 How It Works

The basic flow is:

```text
User avoids a task
        ↓
Starts an Autopsy
        ↓
Describes the task
        ↓
Reports avoidance time
        ↓
Identifies what they did instead
        ↓
Selects behavioral blockers
        ↓
Answers adaptive follow-up questions
        ↓
Data stored locally
        ↓
Pattern analysis
        ↓
Procrastination DNA
        ↓
Personalized next action
```

## 🧠 Example

Imagine you wanted to start a machine-learning project.

You keep avoiding it for two hours.

The app asks:

**Why didn't you start?**

You select:

```text

😨 Fear of failure
😬 Overwhelmed
```

After several autopsies, the app may discover:

```text
Unclear next step      38%
Fear of failure        27%
Overwhelmed            21%

```

Instead of telling you:

> *“You wasted 2 hours.”*

it tells you:

> **Your procrastination seems to begin when a task feels ambiguous and important.**

That's the core idea behind this project.
